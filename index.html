Overview

Watch It India is an Android application for Indian vintage watch collectors. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how it is stored, and your rights over it.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for advertising profiling.

AI Processing

When you use the scan feature, the front and back images of your watch are sent to Google Gemini (Google LLC) for AI-based identification. Google processes these images to return structured watch data. Images are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Google's data processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Cloud Data Processing Addendum. We do not instruct Google to use your images for model training beyond what is described in their standard terms.

Third-Party Services

Supabase Authentication, database, and file storage. Servers in the AWS ap-south-1 (Mumbai) region.
Google Gemini AI watch identification. Watch images are sent to Google's API per scan.
Firebase Crashlytics Crash reporting and stability diagnostics. Collects device model, OS version, and app state at time of crash.
RevenueCat Subscription management for Collector's Pass. Processes in-app purchase receipts from Google Play.
Google Play App distribution. Subject to Google's own privacy terms.

Data Sharing

We share your data only with the third-party processors listed above, and only to the extent necessary to provide the features of the app. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any other party.

We may disclose data if required to do so by applicable Indian law, a court order, or a government authority.

Marketplace listings and public showcase items you mark as public are visible to other registered users of the app.

Data Retention

  • Your account and collection data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • If you delete your account, your personal data and collection records are deleted within 30 days.
  • Crash logs in Firebase Crashlytics are retained for 90 days.
  • Watch images you delete from your collection are removed from Supabase Storage.

Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your collection data within the app at any time.
  • Deletion: You can delete individual collection items, or delete your entire account and all associated data via the app settings.
  • Portability: You may request an export of your collection data by contacting us.
  • Correction: You can edit your collection records directly in the app.

Children

Watch It India is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+). Your collection is protected by Row-Level Security policies — only you can read or write your own records. Authentication uses OTP-based verification with no stored passwords.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
